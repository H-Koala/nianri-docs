Privacy policy

This policy is published by 何年金 and explains how the DayMemo app and this support website handle information. For privacy questions, contact nj.he@foxmail.com.

Your dates stay on your device. Understand how information is handled, and choose what works for you.

01

Local records and no app account

DayMemo does not require an app account and includes no advertising or behavioral analytics services. Dates, titles, notes, categories and reminder settings are processed on your device and are not uploaded to a developer server. You do not need to provide an email address to record dates.

02

Notification permission and the Lock Screen

Reminders are scheduled as local iOS notifications with your permission. Titles and reminder content may appear on your Lock Screen or in Notification Center. You can change previews, sounds or notification permission in system settings. Turning notifications off does not prevent you from viewing local records.

iOS can deliver already scheduled reminders while the app is closed. iOS controls background refresh, and capacity and system settings affect reminders. DayMemo does not promise that you can leave the app unopened forever or that every reminder will be displayed.

03

On-device storage and backups

Records are stored in an on-device local database. DayMemo does not provide automatic app-level cloud synchronization. Depending on your device settings, system backups may include app data.

You can choose to export a JSON backup to Files, iCloud Drive or another location. Exported files are not encrypted and may contain names, dates and notes. Choose storage locations and recipients carefully. Your chosen storage provider handles files under its own policies.

04

Widgets and Quick Entry (from V0.4)

The app shares a minimal on-device date snapshot with its widget extension, including date rules, titles and language, without notes or purchase details. It is not uploaded to a developer server. Titles may appear on the Home Screen or Lock Screen; you can hide them in Widget Settings. iOS controls when displayed widgets refresh.

Quick Entry reads CSV/TSV files you select or table text you paste, previews and validates it locally, then appends only selected valid dates after confirmation. It preserves existing records. JSON backup import instead replaces the library after confirmation. Your chosen file provider may download selected files from its cloud service under its own policies.

05

Apple purchases and access

Apple's App Store and StoreKit process Pro purchases. The app reads and verifies transaction and entitlement information supplied by Apple to enable purchased features. The developer does not receive your payment card details.

Restore Purchases uses your Apple purchase history and may require Apple account verification. JSON backups do not contain or transfer Pro access. Apple's retention and handling of purchase records are subject to its own terms and privacy policies.

06

Adding an event to Calendar

When you choose Add to Calendar, DayMemo passes that occurrence's title, notes and date to Apple's system editor. You decide whether to save it and which calendar to use. The app does not request full calendar read access or continuously read your calendars.

This adds one event; it is not two-way synchronization. Later edits or deletions in DayMemo do not change or remove the saved Calendar event. Adding it again may create duplicates. Calendar storage and synchronization depend on your chosen calendar and system settings.

07

Retention, export and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app, replace them with an imported backup, or the system removes the relevant app data. You can export a backup in Settings and delete dates individually. Import requires confirmation before replacing the current library; back up anything you wish to keep first.

Deleting the app may remove local records. Offloading an app while keeping its data is different from deleting it. Manage exported files, device backups and saved Calendar events separately in their respective locations. The developer has no copy of your local dates and cannot remotely restore or delete them.

08

Information you choose to email

If you contact nj.he@foxmail.com, 何年金 receives your email address and any message or attachments you choose to send, for replying, troubleshooting and following up on your request. Email providers transmit and store these messages.

Send only information needed to resolve the issue. Do not send Apple account passwords, payment card details or an unredacted complete date backup. Support records are kept only as needed to resolve the issue, provide necessary follow-up or meet legal obligations, then deleted or stripped of identifying information. You can request deletion using the same email address, except for information that must be retained by law.

09

This website and GitHub Pages

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and adds no advertising or behavioral analytics scripts. GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security, including visits made without signing in to GitHub.

These website access records are separate from dates stored inside DayMemo. See the GitHub Privacy Statement linked in the footer for GitHub's handling, retention and rights information. Do not submit sensitive personal information to public GitHub pages.

10

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated, with a revised date, when the app's or website's information handling changes. The policy should reflect the version you use and its actual practices.
