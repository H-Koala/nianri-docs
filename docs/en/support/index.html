Help & support

Find answers about local data, purchases and reminders below. For further help, contact support by email.

Frequently asked questions

Does DayMemo need to stay open for reminders?

iOS delivers reminders already scheduled, so the app does not need to remain open. Regular Gregorian rules can repeat yearly; lunar and special dates require later refreshes. The current app queues at most 60 pending requests in total, potentially including one refresh prompt. This is not a number of dates guaranteed indefinitely. iOS decides when background refresh runs. Check unqueued counts and refresh prompts in Settings, and open the app after travelling across time zones. Focus, Scheduled Summary and permissions can also affect presentation.

How do I restore a Pro purchase?

Use the Apple account that made the original purchase, then choose Restore Purchases in DayMemo's Settings → Pro. A backup restores date data, not purchase access. Follow any message about pending approval, failed verification or a refund. If the problem continues, contact support with a redacted description of the error.

How do the Free, Pro and reminder limits differ?

Free includes up to 10 dates, one reminder option per date, Gregorian and lunar calendars, and backups. Pro expands storage to 1,000 dates and adds custom categories and multiple advance reminders. Both plans share the notification queue capacity. Pro does not remove system or app reminder limits, and storing 1,000 dates does not mean they are all scheduled at once. A change in purchase access does not automatically delete saved data.

How do I add a widget?

From V0.4, save dates in DayMemo, then add a DayMemo widget through the system Home Screen editing menu. Small shows the next date and medium shows up to three. Add the rectangular Lock Screen widget through system Lock Screen customization. Tap to open the corresponding date. Hide titles or request updates in Widget Settings. iOS controls refresh timing; widgets do not send additional notifications.

How do I add dates in bulk?

From V0.4, open Quick Entry from the Home Screen import button or Settings, then choose a CSV/TSV file or paste a table. Required columns are title and date; localized examples are included. Preview and select valid rows before appending. Invalid dates and duplicates cannot be selected. Limits are 1,000 rows and 256 KB, subject to Free/Pro date, category and reminder access. JSON backup import replaces the library instead.

Will dates return automatically after reinstalling or changing devices?

DayMemo has no app account or automatic cross-device sync. Export a backup on the old device and import it on the new one. Whether a system device backup restores app data depends on your settings and restore method. JSON backup import replaces the current library, and additions must fit your current access. The current backup file limit is 16 MiB. Exported files are not encrypted.

Why did an edit or deletion not update Calendar?

Adding to Calendar is a one-time action you confirm, not a synchronized connection. Edit or delete that event in Calendar separately. DayMemo does not automatically create an annual lunar recurrence, and repeating the add action may create duplicate events.

How can I delete data or make a privacy request?

Delete dates individually in DayMemo. Manage exported files, device backups and Calendar events separately where they are stored. The developer has no copy of your local dates and cannot remotely restore or delete them. To request deletion of support email or ask about this policy, contact nj.he@foxmail.com.

Helpful details to include

  • Your DayMemo and iOS versions.
  • Your device model and the steps that led to the issue.
  • What you expected, what happened, and approximately when.
  • For reminder issues, the device’s current time zone and notification permission status. Redact personal information from screenshots.

Do not send Apple account passwords, payment card details or your complete date backup. Include only the information needed to investigate.
